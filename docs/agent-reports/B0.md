## B1 manual reproduction / acceptance script

In capture (compact and wide), reader content/caption, library search, tag and
thread/rename fields, use EN and FA UI, light/dark:

1. Paste `English line.` then a newline and `سلام دنیا!`, another newline and
   `Test (۱۲۳) 😀 می‌نویسم.`. Move the caret to each paragraph, then type punctuation.
2. Verify the active paragraph's first strong letter controls editing direction;
   neutral new paragraphs inherit the previous direction. In read mode each
   paragraph uses its own direction. The other edited paragraphs still share
   Flutter's base direction: this is a mitigation, not an Android native editor.
3. With the Persian keyboard/IME, compose text; Ctrl+Z/Y; Shift+arrows, Home/End,
   Ctrl+Backspace; double/triple click and drag across paragraphs. Focus, raw text,
   selected ranges and composition must survive. Repeat with 10,000 characters.
4. Paste via Ctrl+V while continuing to type; existing async-paste race protection
   must remain. Save/reopen/export: no inserted direction marks and no lost text.
5. Capture a short PNG/GIF sequence showing caret/selection before and after.
   No such device capture has been made in this Linux workspace.

## B2/B3 physical follow-up

- Default close quits. Enable tray retention, move/resize, close: process lives,
  tray Open/left-click and single-instance launch restore compact corner on the
  cursor's monitor; merely focusing a visible window leaves it in place. Remember
  size/position opts out of corner reset. Quit drains writes and exits cleanly.
- Test at 100/150% and on two monitors with taskbars on every available edge.
- Installed AUMID: due in two minutes, open/minimized/hidden/quit; observe exactly
  one toast. Inspect `scheduledReminderDetails` for daily/weekly queue and removal.
- Sleep through a one-off, resume and reopen: recover once. Action Center history
  is best-effort when Windows no longer exposes a dismissed scheduled toast;
  exactly-once delivery across all OS dismissal/sleep/crash states is not proven.
- Click body (running/closed) to open the note; click localized Snooze/Done with
  app running and fully exited. Repeat old action after edit: no mutation. Snooze
  keeps daily/weekly repetition anchored to the snoozed time; Done removes the
  reminder/series, never the note. DND can suppress a banner even after acceptance.
